<Update label="2026-10-04" description="CMS and player fixes — stacking, variables on paths, languages" tags={["bugfix"]}>
  ## Panel stacking survives a reload, choices change variables, readers keep their language

  **CMS editor and publishing:**

  - **Send to Back sticks.** Arranging panels used to write a negative
    z-index, which the export dropped, so after a reload every panel was
    back at the same level. The arrange commands now renumber the page's
    panels to a gapless stack within the format's 0–9999 range, as one undo
    step and one save ([Pages & Panels](/cms/editor/pages-panels)).
  - **Variable changes on a path reach the reader.** Variable changes
    stored on a graph edge — on choices created through MCP or imported
    from a manifest — are now exported as the edge's `action`, the field
    the player applies ([Graph](/cms/editor/graph#transitions-and-edge-effects)).
  - **Imports keep hotspot labels.** Choices of an imported manifest or
    work archive keep their labels and accessibility labels, and edges keep
    their variable changes ([Import](/cms/import#what-gets-imported)).
  - **Page view follows the header language.** Switching the language in
    the editor header now also redraws the balloons in page view, and
    balloons of a panel selected in page view no longer disappear after
    you open another panel.
  - **MCP:** outlines can branch, rejoin and end with `next`, choice labels
    keep every language, and the work's cover no longer counts as an
    unused asset.

  **Player and reader** (CMS preview and read.panelwave.org; in
  `@panelwave/player` from the release after 1.2.0):

  - **`localeChange` reports changes only.** It no longer fires with the
    player's internal `en-US` default on load, which made hosts store
    English as the reader's choice ([Inputs & Outputs](/player/inputs-outputs)).
  - **The reader's language wins over the server default:** `?lang=`, then
    the language picked earlier, then the browser languages, then the work's
    default ([What readers see](/guides/publishing#what-readers-see)).
  - At the 9 px lettering floor, a balloon wider than its panel re-wraps to
    the panel width instead of shrinking its text again.
</Update>

<Update label="2026-10-04" description="Format 1.7.0 additions · @panelwave/types 1.2.0 and @panelwave/cli 1.2.0" tags={["feature"]}>
  ## Choice labels, hotspot buttons, lettering color — and the 1.7.0 tooling

  Four optional fields were folded into **format 1.7.0** before its tooling
  release. Existing manifests stay valid unchanged:

  - **`Edge.label`** — the choice text of a path, shown on the branch
    chooser's buttons ([Graph](/schema/graph#edge)).
  - **`Hotspot.display`** — `auto` | `button` | `area`: show the
    hotspot's label as a button or keep an invisible click area. `auto`
    (default) shows buttons for a choice ([Hotspots](/schema/hotspots#hotspot)).
  - **`BalloonConfig.textColor`** — the lettering color, e.g. amber
    on-screen text on a dark fill ([Speech Bubbles](/schema/speech-bubbles#balloonconfig)).
  - **Variable ids may use underscores**, such as `trust_jonas`
    ([Variables](/schema/variables)).

  Released on npm: **`@panelwave/types` 1.2.0** (types the 1.7.0 fields)
  and **`@panelwave/cli` 1.2.0** (bundles the 1.7.0 schema; `upgrade`
  targets 1.7.0). Update with `npm install -g @panelwave/cli@latest`. The
  current versions of all packages are listed under
  [Versioning → Current releases](/schema/versioning#current-releases).

  - **CMS:** exports the new fields — **Shown as** on hotspots
    ([Hotspots](/cms/editor/hotspots#naming-and-accessibility)), the **Text**
    color in the Balloon Style panel, choice labels from the graph editor —
    and accepts variable ids with underscores.
  - **Player:** `@panelwave/player` 1.2.0 already reads `Edge.label`;
    `display` and `textColor` follow in the release after 1.2.0 and are
    live in the CMS preview and on read.panelwave.org.
</Update>

<Update label="2026-10-04" description="Reader, player and MCP — readable branching stories on every screen" tags={["feature", "bugfix"]}>
  ## Findings from building a branching work entirely through MCP

  In the PanelWave reader and the CMS preview; in `@panelwave/player` from
  the release after 1.2.0:

  - **Choices are visible.** The hotspots of a choice show their labels as
    buttons, also on video panels. At a decision, **Next** opens the choice
    dialog instead of picking a path ([Branch choices](/player/reader-interface#branch-choices)).
  - **Lettering scales with the page** and never renders below 9 px; balloons
    stay inside their panel. On phones, page view uses the full width.
  - **The reader starts in the reader's language**: the browser language when
    the work has it, a language picked earlier on that device, or `?lang=`
    in the link ([What readers see](/guides/publishing#what-readers-see)).

  [MCP server](/cms/integrations/mcp-overview):

  - **Pages per format.** A work created from an outline gets one panel per
    page on phones and several on larger formats (4 on tablet, desktop and
    landscape print, 6 on portrait print, 8 on big screens), with a new page
    at each choice. **`pw_pages_compose`** re-lays out one format of a
    chapter after a dry run and your confirmation, and warns about balloons
    that would overlap.
  - **`pw_assets_reprocess`** queues thumbnails, image sizes and video
    renditions again for assets that stayed *processing*.
  - Balloons created through MCP are sized to their text in the work's font;
    choices keep their labels and variable changes.
  All tools: [Tool reference](/cms/integrations/tool-reference).
</Update>

<Update label="2026-10-04" description="CMS — swap a panel's picture for its video version" tags={["feature", "bugfix"]}>
  ## Video backgrounds

  - **Alt-drop swaps the picture.** Hold <kbd>Alt</kbd> while dropping an
    image *or a video* onto a panel: the background layer shows the new
    asset, keeps its place at the bottom, takes its shape, and the panel is
    refitted in every format. Before, only images could be swapped. A video
    dropped without Alt was added as a small layer on top, and deleting the
    old picture left the panel without a background.
  - **Set as Background** in the layer context menu makes an image or video
    layer the panel's background. The previous background is hidden, not
    deleted. It also repairs a panel whose background layer was deleted.
  - A one-time hint after a plain drop onto a panel with a background
    explains both ways.
  - **Panel view lays out a video panel correctly.** A panel with a video
    background used to be shown as if it had no picture: full frame, the
    video squeezed into a strip, bubbles and hotspots out of place.

  See [The panel background](/cms/editor/layers#the-panel-background).
</Update>

<Update label="2026-10-04" description="Format 1.7.0 — edges into another chapter are official" tags={["feature"]}>
  ## Edges and hotspot jumps may cross chapters

  An edge's `to` and a hotspot `goTo`'s `to` may name a panel of **any
  chapter** of the work — a chapter transition, or a jump from the last
  chapter into an endings chapter. The reader continues in the target's
  chapter. `entry` and every edge's `from` stay within the chapter that
  holds the graph. Earlier wording required `to` to be in the same chapter;
  manifests don't change, and existing ones stay valid.

  The schema descriptions, `@panelwave/types` and the player's types say
  so; see [Graph](/schema/graph) and
  [Moving between chapters](/concepts/graph-navigation#moving-between-chapters).
</Update>

<Update label="2026-10-03" description="Player — choices in page view, navigation into another chapter" tags={["feature", "bugfix"]}>
  ## Page view follows the reader's path

  In the PanelWave reader and the CMS [preview](/cms/preview); in
  `@panelwave/player` from the release after 1.2.0.

  - **Choices in page view.** Panels that only some options of a choice lead
    to are grey placeholders until the reader picks an option; then that
    option's path appears. Pages made up only of panels off the chosen path
    are skipped when turning pages, in both directions and across chapters
    ([Choices in page view](/player/reader-interface#choices-in-page-view)).
  - **Into another chapter.** An edge or hotspot jump to a panel of another
    chapter now switches to that chapter, so **Next** continues there. Before,
    the player kept the old chapter and showed the new chapter's entry panel
    again ([Moving between chapters](/concepts/graph-navigation#moving-between-chapters)).
</Update>

<Update label="2026-10-03" description="CMS — whole-work graph, working hotspots and choices, balloon presets, arrange by artwork" tags={["feature", "bugfix"]}>
  ## Building a large branching story

  **Graph Editor** ([Graph](/cms/editor/graph)):

  - **Whole work (all chapters)** in the Chapter dropdown shows the entire
    story: one row per chapter, the links between chapters, and dashed grey
    links where a chapter end continues with the next chapter.
  - A selected panel lists its **Outgoing** edges (condition and delete per
    edge), and **Connect to…** adds an edge to any panel of the work, grouped
    by chapter. Panels of other chapters appear as *↗ Title (Chapter)* nodes.
  - Titles wrap onto up to three lines; from 80% zoom each node shows its
    panel's picture. The mouse wheel zooms toward the pointer (2%–300%).
  - **Simulate** keeps the current panel in view, and a long path scrolls so
    the choices and **Step back** stay visible.
  - Validation accepts edges into another chapter and counts panels entered
    from another chapter as reachable.
  - Exported and published manifests keep edges into another chapter; they
    were dropped before.

  **Hotspots** ([Hotspots](/cms/editor/hotspots)):

  - Hotspots built in the editor were never saved into the work: their
    actions were lost and the hotspot was left out of the export. They now
    save, export and open for editing again. **Navigate** with **Set
    Variables** is published as one jump that records the choice.
  - **Navigate** picks its target from a list of the work's panels, grouped
    by chapter, and stores the panel itself, so renaming a friendly ID no
    longer breaks a jump.
  - **Visible If** uses the JSON Logic Rule Builder.
  - The canvas label shows the label text (it read *[object Object]*), and a
    panel's hotspots no longer stay drawn over the page view.
  - The action, mutation and condition dialogs are readable in dark mode.
  - New guide: [Building a choice](/cms/editor/hotspots#building-a-choice).

  **Speech bubbles** ([Speech Bubbles](/cms/editor/speech-bubbles)):

  - **Balloon presets**: define named styles under Work Properties →
    Typography → Balloon Presets and give a bubble one with **Style preset**.
  - **Fit height to text** (one bubble) and **Fit All Balloons to Text**
    (chapter context menu) size balloons for their longest language.
  - The first image of a panel no longer moves its bubbles and hotspots off
    the artwork, and resizing a caption that sits on a panel edge no longer
    runs away.

  **Pages and panels** ([Pages & Panels](/cms/editor/pages-panels)):

  - **Arrange Panels by Artwork** (page) and **Arrange All Pages by Artwork**
    (chapter) pack the panels into justified rows that keep each picture's
    proportions.
  - **Frame Shape** in the panel inspector crops a panel to a fixed ratio,
    for all page formats at once.

  **Script import and localization**:

  - [Script Import](/cms/script-import#follow-my-script) offers **Follow my
    script**: one panel per scene heading and a chapter at every
    `# Section`, with no AI call.
  - The [Localization Workshop](/cms/localization) extracts panel
    descriptions and hotspot labels. **Approve MT (N)** confirms all shown
    machine translations at once and writes them into the work.
  - **Translate untranslated** works through a whole book: strings go to
    DeepL in batches of 50 and are retried when DeepL asks to slow down
    (before, it stopped after about 75 strings).
  - The **Locales** button waits until the work's locales have loaded.
    Before, adding a language too early could replace the work's default
    language.
</Update>

<Update label="2026-10-02" description="Reader · Player — links to every page and panel, page colors, fullscreen, video pacing" tags={["feature", "bugfix"]}>
  ## Pages and panels you can link to, and pages that look like the CMS

  In the PanelWave reader and the CMS [preview](/cms/preview); in
  `@panelwave/player` from its next release. Details in
  [Reader Interface](/player/reader-interface).

  - **Cover first, then the page.** A work opens on its cover and continues
    in page view. A reader returning to a bookmark sees the cover too, with
    their page behind it.
  - **A link for every page and panel.** The address bar follows the reader:
    `?page=pg-D3` in page view, `?panel=ch1-p022` in panel view. Opening such
    a link starts right there, without the cover. A page link opened on
    another kind of screen opens the page with the same panels. New player
    output `locationChange`, inputs `initialPageId` and `initialViewMode`,
    and the `locationUrl` / `parseLocationSearch` helpers
    ([inputs and outputs](/player/inputs-outputs)).
  - **Sharing that works.** The **Share** dialog shows the link to the page or
    panel on screen (it was empty), and its QR code is drawn in the browser
    instead of loading as a broken image from a third-party service.
  - **Language switch for translated works.** The toolbar offers every
    language the work's text is translated into, not just the languages
    listed in the work's settings.
  - **The page's own color.** The page background color fills the gutters in
    page view and the frame around the panel in panel view, and it switches
    with the page. The light-gray border around the page is gone; the page
    runs edge to edge.
  - **Navigation.** Page view shows the previous/next arrows on hover like
    panel view. Double-click a panel to open it large, and double-click again
    to return to the page.
  - **Video pacing.** Image panels between two videos on a page pause the
    video sequence for their display duration, so the art gets read before
    the next video starts. Hovering or clicking a video that is waiting plays
    it.
  - **Toolbar.** A solid bar with a clear top line, a **Fullscreen** button,
    a *Made with PanelWave* credit and a help link to this documentation.
  - **Fixes.** Balloon text is centered in its balloon (it could sit high
    when the lettering font loaded late), also in the CMS editor. The browser
    tab shows the PanelWave icon.
</Update>

  - **Fixes on the Team page.** Revoking or resending an invitation, removing

  - **Translations add their language.** Text translated into a language the

<Update label="2026-10-02" description="Reader · Player — page layout per screen, cover first, the author's autoplay timing" tags={["feature", "bugfix"]}>
  ## Published works read better on every screen

  Live on [read.panelwave.org](/guides/publishing#what-readers-see) and in the
  CMS [preview](/cms/preview). In `@panelwave/player` from its next release.

  - **The page layout that fits the screen.** Page view shows the page
    sequence of the authored [page format](/cms/works#output) that suits the
    device (the phone layout on a phone, the big-screen layout on a 4K
    display) and re-picks it when the window is resized. The page takes its
    format's aspect ratio and fills the screen; it was a fixed 16:9 box of at
    most 1400 px before. Paging continues into the next and previous chapter.
    Embedders can force a format with the new `pageFormat` input
    ([inputs](/player/inputs-outputs)).
  - **Cover first.** Reading from the beginning opens on the work cover
    (`meta.cover`, otherwise `extras.cover`). Next leaves it, and previous on
    the first panel or page returns to it. It also heads the thumbnail strip
    and the table of contents. New input `showCover` (default `true`).
  - **The author's timing.** Autoplay holds each panel for its
    [display duration](/cms/editor/animations#panel-timing-display-duration)
    (in page view, the sum over the page's panels). Picking a speed in the
    toolbar switches to the reader's seconds per panel, and **Author** switches
    back. Autoplay stops at the end of the work and no longer stalls after a
    video panel. `secondsPerPanel` now applies only to panels without a
    duration and to the cover, until the reader picks a speed.
  - **Thumbnails with artwork.** The thumbnail strip and the table of contents
    show each panel's artwork in reading order, and the strip puts a title
    card between chapters. In page view a thumbnail or contents entry opens
    the page that shows the panel (it did nothing before), and the contents
    list only the pages of the format on screen. Extras linked by URL only
    show that image as their thumbnail.
  - **Faster page turns.** Preloading warms the next panels or pages in
    reading order, also into the next chapter, at the size they will be shown,
    plus the first panel while the cover is up. Images are warmed so the
    browser reuses them, and this also works on storage without CORS headers.
  - **Chrome.** The button that opens the toolbar shows the PanelWave icon,
    and the reader no longer shows a "Made with PanelWave" pill. Player
    controls use the UI font (Barlow, override with `--pw-font-ui`).
  - **Ames Pro lettering.** Balloons set in Ames Pro now render in Ames Pro
    on read.panelwave.org and in the CMS preview, under PanelWave's font
    license (PanelWave's own domains only). The open-source player and
    exported works still do not include the font: self-hosters license it from
    Blambot, otherwise balloons fall back to Comic Neue
    ([Balloon renderer](/player/balloon-renderer)).
</Update>

<Update label="2026-10-01" description="CMS · Public reader — publish to read.panelwave.org, review links, embed, QR codes and sharing" tags={["feature", "security"]}>
  ## Readers open your work on a normal web page

  Publishing now puts your work on the PanelWave reader at
  **read.panelwave.org**. Readers need no account and no app. Start with the
  new [Publishing and Sharing guide](/guides/publishing).

  - **Three targets** in the **Publish Work** wizard: **Public** (anyone with
    the link, at `read.panelwave.org/your-team/your-work`), **Review link**
    (a private link for reviewers that stays the same on every republish) and
    **Internal preview** (your team only, inside the CMS).
  - **Frozen snapshots.** Every publish freezes the work as it is. Later
    edits stay private until you publish again, and asset links are signed
    each time the work is opened.
  - **Public address.** Set the team part on the [Team page](/cms/account#team-name-and-public-address)
    and the work part in **Work Properties → Metadata → Public address**,
    which checks availability as you type. Renamed addresses forward old links,
    so shared links and embeds keep working.
  - **Share it.** The success screen and the new **Publishing** tab of Work
    Properties show the reader link, an iframe **embed code**, a **QR code**
    (PNG or SVG) and a share menu (X, Facebook, Reddit, e-mail). Links unfurl
    into a preview card with title, description and cover. The **My Works**
    card menu gains **Open public page** and **Copy reader link**.
  - **Review links** are excluded from search engines and can be revoked
    with **Revoke & new link**.
  - **Unpublish, history and restore.** The Publishing tab lists the latest
    10 publishes. **Restore** makes an earlier one live again without
    touching your editor content, and **Unpublish** takes a target offline.
  - **Paid content stays on the server.** For anonymous readers, panels behind
    a subscription or purchase rule arrive as locked placeholders
    ([`"x-locked": true`](/schema/extensions)) with no artwork, text or audio.
    Locked extras keep only their title. Age gates are enforced in the reader.
    Review links show the full work but still ask for the age
    ([Monetization](/cms/monetization#how-the-paywall-reaches-readers)).
  - **Internal preview is team-only.** The [Preview](/cms/preview) gains a
    **Working copy | Published preview** switch that shows the last Internal
    preview publish. Public `/preview/{token}` manifest links are retired, and
    so is the preview's **Copy URL** button. To show a work outside the CMS,
    publish it.
  - **Deleting an asset** that a live publish still shows now says so in the
    confirmation: readers see missing artwork until you republish
    ([Assets](/cms/assets)).
  - **AI assistants:** `pw_publish_preview` now publishes the review link and
    returns it, and `pw_publish_rollback` restores the frozen release of an
    earlier publish ([tool reference](/cms/integrations/tool-reference#publishing)).
</Update>

<Update label="2026-10-01" description="Player · paywall and age gates hold in every view; locked placeholders" tags={["bugfix", "security", "feature"]}>
  ## Gated panels stay gated, whichever way the reader gets there

  Live in the CMS preview and on read.panelwave.org. In `@panelwave/player`
  from its next release. Details: [Paywall & entitlements](/player/paywall-entitlement).

  - **Page view and canvas view no longer show gated panels.** The paywall was
    only checked when moving panel by panel, so a page, or a panel revealed on
    the canvas, showed content the reader had not paid for or confirmed their
    age for. Locked panels now render as a placeholder and repaint when access
    changes.
  - **The first panel is gated too:** on the initial load, on chapter jumps
    from the table of contents, and when resuming a bookmark or
    `initialPanelId`. The age gate or paywall opens over the locked
    placeholder; confirming the age reveals it in place.
  - **Age requirements combine** across every rule that applies to a panel,
    so a chapter's free preview or a free panel rule never skips a work-wide
    age gate. The gate asks for the highest minimum age. The purchase and
    subscription part keeps its precedence (panel, then chapter, then work).
    The CMS preview and entitlement simulator follow the same rule.
  - **No leaks around the gate.** A gated panel's audio stays silent until the
    lock lifts, and the assets of locked panels are no longer preloaded.
  - **Locked placeholders** for panels a server stripped
    ([`"x-locked": true`](/schema/extensions), format 1.7). They stay locked
    whatever the rules say. In page view the placeholder is a button that
    opens the age gate or paywall for that panel in place (new viewport output
    `lockedPanelActivate`).
  - **Chapters without graph edges** follow their reading order instead of
    stopping on the entry panel, and `work_complete` fires on the last panel
    ([Graph](/schema/graph)).
  - **Note for embedders:** a custom `entitlementAdapter` decides navigation,
    not rendering. Do not rely on it to hide content: strip paid content on
    the server, or pass an `entitlementSnapshot`.
  - **Demo app:** `?entitlements=free,premium,purchased:<productId>,age-verified`
    sets the reader's entitlements, using the same syntax as the CMS preview's
    simulator ([Demo app](/player/demo-app)).
</Update>

<Update label="2026-10-01" description="CMS · team rename, large archive uploads, storage cleanup for deleted works" tags={["feature", "bugfix"]}>
  ## Smaller improvements and fixes

  - **Rename teams.** Owners and admins rename a team with the pencil next to
    its name on the [Team page](/cms/account#team-name-and-public-address).
    Personal teams that all start as *"Your Name's Team"* can now be told apart.
  - **Large archives upload reliably.** [Archive imports](/cms/import#importing-a-work-archive)
    above 16 MiB go up in 8 MiB parts, and each part is retried up to five
    times with increasing pauses. A single long upload used to fail on
    throttled storage with a misleading CORS error. The admin console's work
    transfer uses the same upload.
  - **Archive imports keep covers and portraits.** The work cover, character
    portraits and extras images of an imported archive now point at the
    imported files. Before, they arrived broken or missing.
  - **Deleted works free their storage.** When you delete a work permanently,
    its files are removed from storage in a nightly cleanup 7 days later.
    Files another work still uses are kept ([Works](/cms/works#trash-restoring-and-deleting)).
</Update>

<Update label="2026-10-01" description="Format 1.7.0 — empty graph edges, x- extensions on panels and extras" tags={["feature"]}>
  ## Single-panel chapters are valid, and panels can carry `x-` fields

  **Format 1.7.0** (relaxing, backward-compatible with 1.6.0 — existing
  manifests stay valid unchanged):

  - **`Graph.edges` may be empty.** A chapter without edges, such as a
    single-panel chapter, is valid and follows its reading order. The property
    stays required. [Graph](/schema/graph).
  - **`x-` extension fields on panels and extras blocks**, in addition to the
    manifest root — for example a server-side paywall view marks stripped
    panels `"x-locked": true`. [Extensions](/schema/extensions).

  Details: [Versioning](/schema/versioning).

  - **CMS:** exports and publishes 1.7.0 and imports formats 1.0–1.7. A
    chapter without links between its panels is written with an entry-only
    graph (`edges: []`), and the [public reader](/guides/publishing) marks
    panels a reader has not unlocked with `"x-locked": true`.
  - **Player:** renders 1.7 — chapters without edges follow their reading
    order, and `x-locked` panels show a locked placeholder
    ([Paywall & entitlements](/player/paywall-entitlement)). Live in the CMS
    preview and on read.panelwave.org; in `@panelwave/player` from its next
    release.
  - **SDK:** `@panelwave/cli` bundles the 1.7.0 schema and `@panelwave/types`
    types the `x-` fields on `Panel` and extras blocks, both from their next

<Update label="2026-07-12" description="Format 1.3" tags={["feature"]}>
  ## Format 1.3 — reusable style presets & implicit speech toggle

  Additive, backward-compatible release: every valid 1.0–1.2 manifest remains valid unchanged.

  - **Reusable style presets** — like CSS classes for the manifest. Named text
    styles in `settings.typography.textStyles` (referenced by
    `TextLayer.styleRef`) and named balloon presets in
    `settings.typography.balloonPresets` (referenced by
    `SpeechBubble.styleRef`). Shared styling is defined once and edited in one
    place; the balloon cascade becomes work → character → **preset** → bubble.
    Sample 11 replaced 104 identical caption style blocks and 79 repeated
    balloon overrides with one text style and six balloon presets.
  - **The speech toggle is implicit** — every speech bubble is inherently
    subject to the reader's speech toggle (initial state:
    `settings.ui.speechDefault`). The per-bubble
    `visibleIf: {"var": "prefs.speech"}` boilerplate is obsolete; `visibleIf`
    is reserved for story logic, and a forgotten boilerplate line can no
    longer make a bubble immune to the toggle.
  - **Player** — text layers and balloons resolve `styleRef` presets; the
    toolbar speech toggle is functional and seeded from `speechDefault`.
  - **CMS** — presets are exported with the manifest, `styleRef` round-trips
    through the editor, validation warns about dangling preset references,
    and legacy `prefs.speech` boilerplate is stripped on export.

  Details: [Settings](/schema/settings), [Speech Bubbles](/schema/speech-bubbles), [Layers](/schema/layers), [Versioning](/schema/versioning).
</Update>
