<Update label="2026-09-29" description="Player · Unreleased (1.2.0) — Angular 21/22, ngx-translate 18, panel ids on panelChange" tags={["feature"]}>
  ## The player installs on Angular 21 and 22 and ngx-translate 18, and `panelChange` tells you which panel

  These changes are merged on `master` but **not yet released** to npm at the
  time of writing; they will ship as `@panelwave/player` **1.2.0**. The current
  npm release is 1.1.0.

  - **Angular 21 and 22.** The `@angular/core` / `@angular/common` peer range is
    now `^20.0.0 || ^21.0.0 || ^22.0.0` (1.1.0 declares `^20.0.0`, which makes
    npm refuse the install on newer apps). The package is still compiled with
    Angular 20 in partial mode; fresh Angular 20.3, 21.2 and 22.2 apps are
    verified in CI — strict-peer install, production build, headless render,
    zone.js and zoneless. No library code changed.
    [Installation](/player/installation#requirements).
  - **ngx-translate 18.** `@ngx-translate/core` is `^17.0.0 || ^18.0.0`. Register
    it with `provideTranslateService({ loader: { provide: TranslateLoader,
    useClass: YourLoader } })` — this form works on 17 and 18, while
    `TranslateModule.forRoot()` no longer exists in 18 and 18's
    `provideTranslateLoader(Class)` breaks minified production builds for a
    plain class. [Application setup](/player/installation#application-setup),
    [troubleshooting](/help-center/troubleshooting/player-embedding#build-or-injection-errors-on-startup).
  - **`panelChange` carries the panel id.** The payload is the new exported
    `PlayerPanelChangeEvent` — `{ panel, chapter, panelId, previousPanelId? }`.
    `panelId` is the panel's key in `chapter.panels` (`Panel.id` is optional
    in the format and usually absent, so hosts no longer look it up by object
    identity); `previousPanelId` is unset for the first panel after a load or
    reload. Additive: `panel` and `chapter` are unchanged. `panel` is the
    manifest panel as written — variants are not resolved in it (the previous
    docs said otherwise). [Inputs & Outputs](/player/inputs-outputs#outputs),
    [Saving Progress](/player/saving-progress#automatic-resume).
  - **Consumer smoke test.** `npm run smoke:consumer -- --angular <major>`
    packs the library into a fresh app of that major and renders the demo
    work; CI runs it for 20, 21 and 22.
    [Development & Contributing](/player/development).
  - **Docs.** The `Panel` example in the package README read `$event.panel.id`,
    which did not compile; it now reads `$event.panelId`. The setup notes
    mention `allowedCommonJsDependencies` for `json-logic-js`.
</Update>

<Update label="2026-09-29" description="CMS · Move to…, cross-format panel deletion, canvas rotate and phone ribbon, nudging fixes" tags={["feature", "bugfix"]}>
  ## Move chapters, pages and panels for real, delete panels without losing them elsewhere, rotate on the canvas board, and see whether a canvas reads on a phone

  - **"Move to…" works** for chapters, pages and panels in the structure tree
    (it used to say *coming soon*). The dialog offers only valid destinations:
    a position among the chapters, another chapter for a page, a page of
    another chapter for a panel. A page takes its panels along; a page or
    panel moved to another chapter is taken off the source chapter's other
    formats' pages, its canvas layout and its graph — and **Undo** puts all
    of that back. Dragging a panel onto another chapter's page now goes
    through the same path (it used to leave the panel's chapter, other pages
    and edges dangling). [Move to…](/cms/editor/pages-panels#move-to).
  - **Rename** in the panel context menu; **collapsed chapters stay
    collapsed** across renames, new panels and page reloads (per work, for
    the browser session). [Context menus](/cms/editor/pages-panels#context-menus).
  - **Canvas delete keeps panels other formats still place.** Deleting a
    panel on the page canvas that another page or format still uses only
    removes it from the open page, with a toast saying where it is still
    used; the tree's **Delete Panel** deletes everywhere and its confirmation
    lists the affected pages and formats. Both are one undo step; the legacy
    page references of older works are cleaned and restored too.
    [Deleting panels](/cms/editor/pages-panels#deleting-panels).
  - **Rotate handle on the Canvas Layout Board.** A knob above the selected
    panel rotates it around its origin, **Shift** snaps to 15°; the inspector
    gets a **Rotation (°)** field and **Reset rotation**. Saved with the
    placement and rendered by the player.
    [Canvas Layout Board](/cms/editor/canvas-layout#the-board).
  - **Phone ribbon.** A toolbar toggle overlays the column a portrait phone
    reads and judges the layout **column-friendly** or not, live: panels that
    leave the band and trail steps that read upward are outlined, and a
    verdict chip counts the phone screens. View-only, remembered per browser.
    [Phone ribbon](/cms/editor/canvas-layout#phone-ribbon-will-it-read-on-a-phone).
  - **Nudging fixed.** Arrow keys moved a layer 3 px and Shift+Arrow 30 px
    because the key reached the editor three times; it is 1 px and 10 px
    again, and **speech bubbles and hotspots** nudge too (each an undo step).
    The same fix stops Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Shift+Z from undoing twice, and
    **Ctrl+D** makes one copy instead of two.
    [Moving and resizing](/cms/editor/canvas#moving-and-resizing),
    [Keyboard Shortcuts](/cms/editor/shortcuts#moving-elements).
  - **Preview explains empty works.** A work with no chapters, or none with
    panels, shows *Nothing to preview yet* and what to add instead of a raw
    player error; chapters without panels are left out of the preview and
    counted under the device frame. [Preview](/cms/preview#opening-the-preview).
  - **Accessibility and speed.** Higher-contrast text on the editor header's
    yellow accent, a proper tab list for the editor tabs, accessible names on
    editor, timeline, canvas, team and export controls; the Characters page
    serves thumbnails instead of the original portraits, work cards reserve
    their 16:9 cover space, and the profile lists the 10 newest sessions with
    **Show all**. [Characters](/cms/characters), [Works](/cms/works),
    [Active sessions](/cms/account#active-sessions).
</Update>

<Update label="2026-09-29" description="CMS · Read-only works everywhere, editable preload, bubble audio in archives, page-import fixes" tags={["feature", "bugfix"]}>
  ## Read-only works hide their write actions, the preload setting is yours to edit, archives carry bubble audio, and page imports settle their credits

  - **Read-only works, everywhere.** A work above the plan limit or of a
    suspended team now shows the **Read-only** banner in the Backstage tools
    as well as the editor, and hides or disables every write action instead
    of failing it: no uploads, folder actions, new chapters, drag reorders or
    panel adds in the editor; no add / edit / delete / reorder in Characters,
    Extras, Localization, Audio and Paywall. Any write that still slips
    through is refused with one *Read-only* toast. Export, archive, trash and
    comments are unaffected.
    [Read-only works](/cms/editor/layout#read-only-works), [Plans & Limits](/cms/plans#read-only-works).
  - **Editable preload settings.** The inspector's **Perf** tab's preload block
    is a form — strategy (None / Lookahead / Aggressive), panels ahead (0–20),
    parallel downloads (1–16). Changes update the estimates at once, are
    saved to the work and exported as `settings.preload`; JSON and archive
    imports restore them (out-of-range values are dropped).
    [The Performance tab](/cms/editor/layout#the-performance-tab), [Import](/cms/import#what-gets-imported).
  - **Work archives carry speech-bubble audio.** Uploaded voice lines and TTS
    output per language travel in the archive with their files and are
    re-attached to the same bubbles on import; tracks whose bubble or file is
    missing are skipped and counted. Archives made before 28 September 2026
    carry none. [Archive export](/cms/export#archive-export),
    [Importing a work archive](/cms/import#importing-a-work-archive).
  - **Page Import.** The import step's progress bar now moves (it sat at 2 %
    until done); the upscale credit reservation is settled after every import,
    including when upscaling could not run; **Cancel import** during the
    import step really stops it and removes the empty new work it had
    created; **Discard** releases the reservation; and sessions idle for 30
    days are discarded by the daily cleanup with their credits released and
    an empty new work removed. Upscales also run in parallel now.
    [Running an import](/cms/page-import#running-an-import),
    [Upscaling and credits](/cms/page-import#upscaling-and-credits).
</Update>

<Update label="2026-09-27" description="Format · 1.6.0 — paywall product lists and product catalogue" tags={["feature"]}>
  ## A paywall rule can name every product that unlocks it, and readers see each product's name and price

  - **`requiredProductIds` on paywall rules.** A purchase rule can list every
    product that unlocks it. Owning any one of them is enough, and the player
    offers one Buy option per product.
    [Paywall → Product lists](/schema/paywall#product-lists-requiredproductids).
  - **`paywall.products`.** Describes the products and subscription tiers that
    rules reference by id: localized `name` and `description`, `price`, `type`.
    The player labels each Buy / Subscribe option with the product's name,
    description and price in the reader's language, falling back to the rule's
    name and price. [Paywall → Products](/schema/paywall#products-paywallproduct).
  - **Additive.** Every 1.5 manifest stays valid. [Versioning](/schema/versioning).
  - **CMS export.** Rules export all their unlocking products (previously only
    the first), and each product's name, description and price go into
    `paywall.products`. `requireEntitlement` still names the first product, so
    older players keep working. Import accepts formats 1.0–1.6.
    [How the paywall reaches readers](/cms/monetization#how-the-paywall-reaches-readers).
  - **Tooling.** Released on npm: `@panelwave/types` 1.1.0 and `@panelwave/cli` 1.1.0
    (bundles and targets 1.6.0), `@panelwave/player` 1.1.0 (labels Buy / Subscribe
    options from `paywall.products`). CLI 1.0.0 still validates against 1.5.0 and
    rejects the new fields — update with `npm install -g @panelwave/cli@latest`.
    [CLI](/schema/cli).
</Update>

<Update label="2026-09-27" description="Player 1.1.0 · Live inputs, paywall scopes, Buy / Subscribe, age plus entitlement" tags={["feature", "bugfix", "breaking"]}>
  ## Inputs apply after init, paywall scopes and age checks work as specified, and the overlay offers Buy / Subscribe

  Released on npm as `@panelwave/player` **1.1.0** (`npm install @panelwave/player@latest`).

  - **Inputs can change after init.** A new `manifest` / `manifestUrl` reloads the
    work (position, non-persistent variables, overlays, variant overrides and
    autoplay reset; the previous analytics session is closed), and `reload()` is
    public. A new `entitlementSnapshot` re-evaluates the gates (removing it makes
    the reader anonymous). `locale`, `viewModeOverride`, `showToolbar`,
    `autoplay`, `secondsPerPanel` and `reducedMotion` apply live.
    [Inputs & Outputs](/player/inputs-outputs).
  - **`autoplay` input works.** It starts autoplay once the work is ready; the
    reader's toggle takes over from there.
  - **Table of contents and thumbnails with `manifestUrl`.** Both now list the
    work however it is loaded. [Quickstart: Embed](/player/quickstart-embed).
  - **Chapter and extras paywall scopes.** A `chapter` rule gates only the
    chapter in `refId` (its free preview counted within it) and wins over a work
    rule for its panels. An `extras` rule never gates panels; it locks its extras
    item. New exports `isExtraLocked`, `chapterOrderFromManifest` and
    `PaywallService.isExtraLocked()`.
    [How rules are evaluated](/player/paywall-entitlement#how-rules-are-evaluated).
  - **Age on top of payment.** A rule that combines an entitlement with
    `ageGate` / `minimumAge` now requires both: the reader answers the age gate
    first, then sees the paywall. Before, a rule with a custom
    `requireEntitlement` key and an age unlocked after the birth date alone, and
    purchase or subscription rules ignored their age.
    [Age gates](/player/paywall-entitlement#age-gates).
  - **Buy / Subscribe on the paywall overlay.** Options come from the blocking
    rule (its product or tiers, with name, description and price); *Sign in*
    stays. `paywallAction` is now `{ action, gate, productId? }` with `'purchase'`
    and `'subscribe'`, and `PaywallGate` gains `ruleId`, `lockReason` and
    `options`. [Buy and Subscribe options](/player/paywall-entitlement#buy-and-subscribe-options).
  - **Breaking (types): `PurchaseInfo.price` is optional.** Code that reads
    `option.price.amount` must handle a missing price.
  - **Edge mutations wait for the gate.** A move blocked by the paywall or age
    gate no longer changes variables, and canvas taps now apply edge mutations.
    [Mutations](/player/state-and-conditions#mutations).
  - **Gates hold the story.** Arrow keys, `T` and swipes do nothing while the
    paywall or age gate is open; Escape dismisses the paywall or closes the age
    gate and the reader stays put. Keys in a select no longer reach the story.
    [Input methods](/player/reader-interface#input-methods).
  - **Translated gates and chooser.** New UI keys `age_gate.*`,
    `branch_chooser.*` and `paywall.reason_*` (add them if you ship your own
    translation files); month names via `Intl`. [Localization](/player/localization).
  - **Choices only when there is a choice.** The button appears when at least
    two paths are open. [Branch choices](/player/reader-interface#branch-choices).
  - **Bundle budget measured as shipped.** CI checks the 180 KB budget against
    the minified + gzipped bundle, about 106 KB today.
    [Development & Contributing](/player/development).
</Update>

<Update label="2026-09-27" description="CMS · Paywall targets and products, schema-valid paywall export, playable panel audio" tags={["feature", "bugfix"]}>
  ## Gate a single chapter or bonus item, name the product that unlocks it, and publish rules the player can enforce

  - **Chapter and Extras Item rules.** The paywall editor's scopes are now Work
    Level, Chapter, Panel Level and Extras Item; Chapter and Extras Item rules
    need a picked target. A chapter rule's free preview counts panels within that
    chapter; an extras rule locks only its item. Old *Global* rules behave as Work
    Level rules. [Creating a rule](/cms/monetization#creating-a-rule).
  - **Unlocking products and an optional age on any rule.** Purchase rules list
    the products that unlock them (none checked: any purchase), and every rule can
    add a minimum age that readers must pass in addition to paying.
  - **Published rules the player can enforce.** Exported rules are schema-valid:
    chapter, extras and panel rules carry their target, a purchase rule names its
    first unlocking product (the reader's **Buy** option, with the product's price
    as fallback), and the age travels along. Rules with a missing target are left
    out and flagged by the new
    [`PAYWALL_TARGET_MISSING`](/cms/validation-codes#paywall-target-missing) check
    (an error for active rules). [How the paywall reaches readers](/cms/monetization#how-the-paywall-reaches-readers).
  - **The simulator tests your work's own rules** with the player's logic,
    including chapter previews, locked extras items and age plus entitlement.
    [Simulating reader access](/cms/monetization#simulating-reader-access).
  - **Work archives** carry rule targets and unlocking products and remap them in
    the copy.
  - **Panel audio plays in published works.** Exported manifests now include
    panel audio and audio layers with their asset references, so the player can
    play them. [Audio](/cms/editor/audio).
</Update>

<Update label="2026-09-27" description="CMS · Import 1.0–1.5, Logic issues in Preflight, large-upload controls, video soundtrack" tags={["feature", "bugfix"]}>

  - **Frame rate is honoured.** Video exports use the chosen frame rate; every

  - **Privacy dialog corrected.** The in-app privacy summary now matches the
