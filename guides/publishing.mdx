title: Publishing and Sharing
description: Publish your work to a public reader page, send a private review link, set your public address, embed the work on your own site, and share it with a QR code or on social media.
---

When you publish, PanelWave takes a **snapshot** of your work and puts it on the PanelWave reader at **read.panelwave.org**. Readers open a normal web page, no account and no app. Edits you make afterwards stay private until you publish again.

You choose one of three targets in the **Publish Work** wizard:

| Target | Card in the wizard | Who can open it |
|---|---|---|
| **Production** | **Public** | Anyone with the link, at `read.panelwave.org/your-team/your-work` |
| **Staging** | **Review link** | Anyone you send the link to; it stays the same on every republish and you can revoke it |
| **Preview** | **Internal preview** | Only your team, inside the CMS |

<Callout kind="info">
  Paid content is protected. On the public page, locked parts (for example panels behind a subscription or purchase rule from [Monetization](/cms/monetization)) are removed on the server and readers see a lock instead. Age gates are enforced in the reader. Reviewers using a review link see the full work.
</Callout>

## Publish a work

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the wizard">
    In the editor header click **Publish**. The preflight check runs first; fix any errors it lists (see [Validation](/cms/validation)).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a target">
    Choose **Public**, **Review link** or **Internal preview**, then confirm the options and publish.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy your link">
    For **Public** and **Review link** the success screen shows the reader link with **Copy** and **Open**, plus the embed code, a QR code and sharing buttons. For **Internal preview** it offers **Open internal preview**, which opens the work inside the CMS.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You can find the links later in **Work Properties → Publishing**. The embed code, QR code and share menu are shown there for **Public** only; the **Review link** card offers Copy, Open, Revoke & new link and Unpublish. Links are also available in the **More actions** menu of the work card in **My Works** (**Open public page**, **Copy reader link**).

## Your public address

A public link looks like `read.panelwave.org/{team}/{work}`. Both parts are short, URL-safe names (lowercase letters, digits and hyphens).

- **Team address:** set it on the **Team** page (user menu → **Team**), under the team name: **Public address → Edit**. Owners and admins can change it; see [Teams](/cms/account#team-name-and-public-address).
- **Work address:** set it in **Work Properties → Metadata → Public address**. PanelWave checks while you type whether it is free.

If you publish to **Public** before both are set, the wizard says so and offers **Set the public address** and **Team settings**.

<Callout kind="tip">
  You can rename an address any time. Old links keep working and forward readers to the new address, so shared links and embeds do not break.
</Callout>

Some names are reserved (for example `admin`, `api`, `login`) and cannot be used as an address.

## Review links

Use **Review link** to get feedback before going live. Send the link to editors, testers or a publisher; they need no account.

- The link stays **the same** when you republish, so reviewers always see the latest staging publish.
- It is not listed anywhere and is excluded from search engines, but anyone who has the link can open it. Treat it like a password. Review links are for reviewers: there are no social sharing buttons for them, so pass the link on directly to the people who should read it.
- **Revoke & new link** (Work Properties → Publishing) invalidates the old link immediately and gives you a new one.

## Embed the work on your site

Copy the **embed code** from the success screen or the Publishing tab and paste it into your page:

```html
<iframe src="https://read.panelwave.org/your-team/your-work?embed=1" width="800" height="600"
  style="border:0;max-width:100%" allow="fullscreen" loading="lazy" title="Your work title"></iframe>
```

Keep `?embed=1` in the link: it tells the reader it runs inside another page. The reader fills the frame, and readers open its toolbar with the PanelWave icon in the bottom-right corner. Adjust `width` and `height` to fit your layout.

## What readers see

The reader at read.panelwave.org is the open-source [PanelWave Player](/player/overview), so a published work reads the way it does in [Preview](/cms/preview):

- **Cover first.** Reading from the beginning opens on the work cover ([Work Properties → Metadata](/cms/works#metadata), otherwise the cover in [Extras](/cms/extras)). The cover also heads the thumbnail strip and the table of contents.
- **The right page layout for the screen.** In page view the reader picks the [page format](/cms/works#output) you authored that suits the device — the phone layout on a phone, the big-screen layout on a large display — and switches when the window is resized. Formats you have not laid out are skipped.
- **Your timing.** Autoplay holds each panel for the [display duration](/cms/editor/animations#panel-timing-display-duration) you set. Readers can pick their own speed in the toolbar and go back to yours with **Author**.
- **Thumbnails** show each panel's artwork in reading order, with a title card between chapters.
- **The reader's language.** A work with several languages opens in the reader's browser language when the work has it (an Austrian German browser reads the German version), otherwise in the work's default language. A language picked in the toolbar is remembered on that device, and `?lang=de-DE` in a link opens that language.
- **Lettering.** Balloons set in Ames Pro render in Ames Pro on read.panelwave.org and in the CMS preview. A work you export and host yourself needs its own Ames license; without it, those balloons fall back to Comic Neue (see [Balloon renderer](/player/balloon-renderer#fonts-and-ames-pro)).

## QR code and social sharing

- **QR code:** download a **PNG** or **SVG** for posters, flyers or the back of a book. Anyone who scans it lands on the reader.
- **Share menu:** share to X, Facebook, Reddit or by e-mail, or **Copy link**. Links show a preview card with your title, description and cover image when pasted into social networks and chat apps.

## Unpublish, history and rollback

Everything below is in **Work Properties → Publishing**.

- **Unpublish** takes a target offline. A public link then shows a friendly "no longer available" page. Unknown or revoked links show a "not found" page instead.
- **History** lists the latest 10 publishes. **Restore** makes that version live again for readers, so you can undo a bad publish; your editor content is not changed. Restore is only offered when the version is still stored and is not already live.
- Changes to a public page can take up to 5 minutes to reach readers who already have the page cached.

<Callout kind="info">
  Republishing the same target replaces what readers see.
</Callout>
