Characters give your dialogue an identity. Once a speech bubble is assigned to a character, it automatically inherits that character's balloon style, readers can meet the cast through [character sheets](/cms/extras), and generated voice audio can use the character's voice.

Open **Characters** from a work's Backstage rail (group **Cast & Content**), from the work's section grid in the [Works](/cms/works) inspector, or from the work card's menu.

## The cast board

The page is laid out like every Backstage tool: an optional list on the left (the **team library**), the **board** in the middle, and the **inspector** on the right — a column on wide screens, a drawer on narrower ones (<kbd>]</kbd> toggles it, <kbd>[</kbd> the team library, <kbd>?</kbd> the Guide rail).

The board groups your characters by role — **Protagonists → Antagonists → Supporting → Minor → Other** — as portrait tiles that grow with the screen. Characters without a role, for example from a script import or an AI assistant, are filed under **Other**. A tile shows the portrait (or initials on role-coloured paper), the name in the selected language, a role chip, small **coverage dots** for languages that still lack a localized name or bio, how many **bubbles** the character speaks in, a live **balloon sample** in the character's effective style, and a ▶ button that plays the assigned voice. Inactive characters are greyed out and marked *inactive*.

- **Language pills** in the title row switch which localized name and bio the board shows (the work's default language is preselected).
- **Search** by name or bio (<kbd>/</kbd> focuses it), **Board | List | Roster** switch the view.
- **List** is a sortable table for big casts: Portrait · Name · Role · Age · Gender · Voice · Balloon type · Used in · Languages ✓/✗ · Active · Updated. Each row shows a large portrait and, under the name, the character's reference images as a strip of thumbnails (up to five, then *+N*).
- **Roster** is the board without controls and with large portraits — a printable cast sheet (**Print**).

### Reordering and changing roles by drag

Drag a tile within its role section to change the manual order. **Drop it on another section to change the character's role** — an inline **Undo** bar lets you take it back for a few seconds. Without a mouse, <kbd>Alt</kbd>+<kbd>↑</kbd>/<kbd>↓</kbd> moves the focused tile within its group; the arrow keys move the focus, <kbd>Enter</kbd> opens the inspector, <kbd>e</kbd> edits, <kbd>Del</kbd> deletes, <kbd>n</kbd> adds a character.

## Adding a character

**+ Add** offers a blank character, a **template** (archetype starters such as a protagonist, antagonist or narrator that you then customize) and **from the team library**. A new character opens in the inspector as a **draft** — fill in at least the name and click **Create character**.

## Editing in the inspector

Selecting a tile opens the character in the inspector. There is no dialog: **changes save automatically** a moment after you stop typing, and a small *Saving… / Saved* line tells you where you stand. (A name is never saved empty.)

The inspector's sections, top to bottom:

- **Portrait** — drop an image on it, **Upload**, pick **From library** (the [asset picker](/cms/assets#picking-assets-anywhere), pre-filtered to images), paste a URL, or **Remove**. Portraits are shown as thumbnails sized for the board, not the uploaded originals, so a large cast stays quick.
- **Name and localized names** — a compact table with one row per work language and a ✓ where a name exists; empty rows fall back to the main name.
- **Role**, **Age**, **Gender**.
- **Biography** — one tab per language; the default-language tab is the plain bio that everything else falls back to.
- **Reference images** — extra expressions, poses or costume references in a 4-up grid; hover a thumbnail to edit its label, click it for a lightbox. These are CMS-side working material; the exported manifest carries only the portrait.
- **Voice** — the character's default text-to-speech voice plus, optionally, a **voice per language** for dubbing. The picker searches the catalogue, filters by language and gender, shows the provider and plays a ▶ preview; a hint tells you when a voice matches the work's languages. These voices are what the [Audio](/cms/localization#audio-localization-and-text-to-speech) page uses as the cast's voices.
- **Speech balloon** — see below.
- **Used in** — up to eight thumbnails of the panels this character speaks in; click one to open the editor on that panel and bubble.
- **Created / Updated**, the **Active** switch (inactive characters are dimmed and excluded from the roster and the published work), and **Duplicate**, **Share to team** and **Delete**. The delete confirmation names the panels the character speaks in, so you know what you are about to orphan.

### Speech balloon style and presets

The **Speech balloon** section shows the character's balloons exactly as the player will draw them — rendered with the real balloon engine and the work's cascade, scaled up to 1.5× so the sample text is readable while the balloon keeps its real proportions, with a localized sample line in a short and a long variant so you can see how the balloon grows with longer text. A chip line states where the style comes from: *Inherits work default*, *Based on preset X*, or *Overrides: type, fill, tail*.

<Callout kind="info">
**How balloon styles cascade:** work default → balloon preset → character override → per-bubble override. A character override only changes balloons *assigned to that character*, and an individual bubble can still override everything. See [Speech Bubbles](/cms/editor/speech-bubbles).
</Callout>

- **Preset** — pick one of the work's [balloon presets](/cms/editor/speech-bubbles#balloon-presets) (*caption*, *shout*, *whisper*, … defined under Work Properties → Typography) as the character's base. A missing preset is flagged. Presets are a CMS convenience: on publish the preset and the override are flattened into the character's exported balloon style, so the player sees a complete style, not a preset name.
- **Override** — the balloon configurator you know from [Work Properties](/cms/works#typography), scoped to this character: balloon type (Normal, Rectangle, Panel Top, Panel Top-Right, Panel Top-Left, Thought, Shout, Whisper, Connector), colours, tail and typography, with a live preview. **Edit work default** jumps to the work-wide style.

## Linking characters to speech bubbles

Characters connect to dialogue in the editor: select a speech bubble on the canvas and choose the character in the **Speech** inspector tab. From then on the bubble renders with the character's balloon style, and the tile's bubble count and **Used in** list reflect it. See [Speech Bubbles](/cms/editor/speech-bubbles).

## Team character library

Team works get a shared **Team library**, shown in the list on the left (<kbd>[</kbd>). It loads as soon as the page opens; each entry shows when it was last updated and an **In this work** marker if the work already has that character.

- **Share to team** (in the inspector) publishes a **copy** of the character for teammates. After sharing, the library opens with the new entry highlighted. Later edits to your character don't change the copy by themselves: once a copy exists the button reads **Update team copy**, and re-sharing a character with the same name updates the team's copy.
- The inspector also shows the character's library status — who shared it and when — with **Show in library**.
- To bring a shared character into the current work, **drag it onto a role section** of the board — it is imported with that role — or click **Import** on the library entry.

## Import & export

**Export** downloads all characters as a JSON file; **Import** loads a character set from JSON — handy for reusing a cast across works.

## Character sheets for readers

To publish full character sheets as bonus content, create **Character Sheets** in [Extras](/cms/extras) and link each sheet to one or more characters; the sheet shows their portraits.
