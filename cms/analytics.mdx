The **Analytics** board answers one question: *how is my story read?* How often, how far, how long, where readers branch and where they leave. Open a work's Backstage and choose **Analytics** in the **Audience** group (it is also in the work card's menu and the section grid of the [Works](/cms/works) inspector).

<Callout kind="info">
  Metrics are aggregated **nightly** from player reading events. A work only starts collecting data once a **published** version is being read — a brand-new or unpublished work shows *"Share your work to start collecting reads"* with a link to its reader link.
</Callout>

## Choosing what you look at

The controls in the header slice every tile and chart on the board:

- **Range** — **7 days**, **30 days** (default) or **90 days**, or **Custom** with start/end dates. If your [plan](/cms/plans) keeps fewer days of analytics than you asked for, the picker says so (*"Your plan keeps N days of analytics"*) and the range starts where your data starts.
- **Compare with previous period** — fetches the preceding window of equal length: the previous period is drawn dashed on the time chart, and every tile and the selected panel get a **±N %** change badge (**new** where there was nothing before).
- **Device** and **Locale** — filter to one device type or one reading language. The segment applies to the whole board, including panels, paths and the click heatmap. Languages are listed by name with their region.
- **Export ▾** — four CSV files: *summary* (per day plus a total row), *panels* (every panel with all its metrics), *funnel* (every step with its drop-off) and *paths* (panel-to-panel transitions).
- **Refresh**, the help icon, and <kbd>]</kbd> for the inspector.

## The KPI row

Six tiles, each with the headline number, a sparkline for the range and a change badge when comparing:

| Tile | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Reads** | Reading sessions in the range |
| **Readers** | Signed-in readers — the closest thing to unique people |
| **Completion** | Average share of the work a session got through |
| **Reading time** | Combined time readers spent in the work |
| **Avg session** | Average length of a single reading session |
| **Paywall conversion** | Readers who hit a paywall and unlocked it — see [Monetization](/cms/monetization) |

Click a tile for its **per-day detail** in the inspector, with that metric's column highlighted; click a day in the table to select it.

## The zones

### Reads per day

An area chart stacked by **device**; the switch above it shows **Completion** or **Reading time** per day instead. Click a day to see its breakdown in the inspector.

### Reading paths

A flow diagram of how readers actually move through the work — from the cover into the first chapter and on through your branches, with the width of each band showing how many readers took it. This is where a branching story shows its shape: which choice readers prefer, where a branch quietly loses them. The strongest paths are shown; click a panel in the diagram to inspect it.

While a work has no path data yet, the zone shows the linear **reading funnel** instead: every panel in reading order with the sessions that reached it and a **−N %** drop-off label between steps. A steep drop is your clearest signal for a pacing problem, a confusing branch — or a well-placed [paywall boundary](/cms/monetization).

### Panel engagement

The **story heat strip** — every panel as a thumbnail in story order, tinted from cool to hot by views (grey = never viewed). Artists see at a glance which pages lose readers. Hover a thumbnail for its numbers, use <kbd>←</kbd>/<kbd>→</kbd> to walk the strip, click to open the panel in the inspector.

Under the strip, a table lists **all** panels, sortable by position, title, **views**, **viewer days**, **average** and **median dwell**, **time to next panel**, **bounce** (sessions that saw only this panel and left), **exits** and — for choice panels — the **choice split** (which option readers picked, in %). Long dwell can mean a beloved splash page or a panel readers get stuck on; pair it with the paths or the funnel to tell the difference.

### Click heatmap

*Where* readers click on a panel — every tap, whether it landed on a [hotspot](/cms/editor/hotspots) or not. Pick a panel in the selector (every panel, with thumbnail and story position; selecting a panel elsewhere on the board switches the heatmap too). The artwork renders at the zone's full width with hotspot **hits** on the heat ramp and **dead clicks** in violet; hotspots are outlined with their click counts, and the header summarizes the split — e.g. *"120 clicks — 85 on hotspots, 35 dead (29%)"*.

Dead clicks are the interesting part: a hot cluster *outside* every hotspot means readers expect something there to be interactive — consider putting a hotspot where they're already tapping, or making the existing one bigger. A hotspot with few hits despite traffic may be too subtle or oddly placed.

<Callout kind="tip">
  Unlike the other zones, the click heatmap reads **raw click events**, so fresh clicks show up without waiting for the nightly aggregation. Very busy panels are sampled to the most recent 5,000 clicks (the header says *"sampled"* when that happens).
</Callout>

### Audience

**Devices** as a donut, **locales** as bars with the language name and region, and **reads per signed-in reader** as a rough measure of readers coming back. Geographic data is not collected, and a true "returning readers" share is not possible: anonymous readers carry no stable identity.

### Social & paywall

Likes, bookmarks, shares, comments and language switches in the range, with the lifetime counters underneath; and the paywall funnel — **views → unlock clicks → conversions** — with a per-rule table. See [Community](/cms/community) and [Monetization](/cms/monetization).

## The inspector

Whatever you select on the board is explained on the right (<kbd>]</kbd> toggles it; <kbd>?</kbd> opens the Guide rail):

- **A panel** — its thumbnail, story position and chapter, all its metrics, the funnel step (sessions reaching it, dropped off here), the choice distribution, the previous-period row with the views delta when comparing, **Show click heatmap** and **Open in editor**.
- **A funnel step** — the same, for the step's panel.
- **A day** — reads, readers, completion, reading time, average session and panels per read for that date, its device and locale split, and its paywall and social counts.
- **A KPI tile** — the per-day drill-down table.

## Interpreting the numbers

- **A read = a session.** One reader returning three times counts as three reads; **readers** counts signed-in people.
- **Completion** tells you whether readers finish. A high read count with low completion usually means readers drop off — check pacing, chapter length, or where your [paywall preview boundary](/cms/monetization) sits.
- **Compare with previous period** is the quickest way to judge whether a new chapter, a promotion, or a price change moved the needle — look for the change badges.
- Filtering by **device** can reveal layout problems: if phone sessions complete far less than desktop sessions, [preview](/cms/preview) the work on the phone and desktop side by side and check panel legibility.
- Filtering by **locale** shows whether a [translation](/cms/localization) is pulling its weight.
- The **click heatmap** is your interactivity audit: dead-click clusters show where readers *want* a hotspot; a cold hotspot is one they can't find.

<Callout kind="tip">
  Because aggregation runs nightly, don't expect today's reads to appear immediately — check back the next day when judging a release or announcement.
</Callout>

<Callout kind="info">
  **Data retention** — how many days of analytics you can look back depends on your [plan](/cms/plans); the range picker tells you the limit. Raw reading events are deleted after 24 months.
</Callout>
