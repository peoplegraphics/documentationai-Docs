Publishing is how your work leaves the workshop and reaches readers. PanelWave separates two ideas:

1. **Work status** — a label (*Draft*, *Review*, *Published*, *Archived*) that organizes your library and signals where each work is in its lifecycle.
2. **The publish pipeline** — a guided flow that validates the work, packages it into a [manifest](/concepts/manifest), and freezes it as a snapshot that the PanelWave reader serves to readers.

<Callout kind="tip">
  Public links, review links, public addresses, embedding and QR codes are covered in the [Publishing and Sharing guide](/guides/publishing).
</Callout>

## Work statuses

Every work has one of four statuses, shown as a badge on its card in **My Works**:

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Draft** | Work in progress — the default for new works |
| **Review** | Ready for a second pair of eyes |
| **Published** | Finished and released |
| **Archived** | Retired; kept for reference |

To change a status:

- **One work** — use the status dropdown at the bottom of the work's card in the works list. A confirmation toast ("Status set to published") appears, and the badge updates immediately.
- **Several works** — select them with the checkboxes, then use the bulk action bar's status dropdown and **Set status**.

You can also filter the works list by status (**Status: All / Draft / Review / Published / Archived**) to keep an overview of what is live and what isn't.

Setting a work back to **Draft** or **Archived** only changes the label in your library; use **Unpublish** (see the guide) to take a public link offline.

## The guided publish flow

When you publish, the CMS walks you through a four-step wizard titled **Publish Work**: **Preflight → Environment → Options → Confirm**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Preflight Check">
    Validation runs automatically. The wizard summarizes errors, warnings, and info notes. **Errors block publishing** — each is listed with a suggestion for fixing it (see [Validation](/cms/validation)). Warnings don't block you: the wizard shows "Warnings (you can proceed)". Oversized-image errors offer **Optimize** / **Optimize all** right in the wizard — the fix runs in the background and the check re-polls until the errors clear (see [Optimizing oversized images](/cms/validation#optimizing-oversized-images)). Use **Re-validate** after fixing other issues. When everything passes you'll see **"Ready to Publish"**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Environment">
    Choose the target:
    - **Public** — anyone with the link can read it at read.panelwave.org.
    - **Review link** — anyone with the link; stable across republishes, and you can revoke it.
    - **Internal preview** — only your team, inside the CMS.

    Details for each target, and how to share them, are in the [Publishing and Sharing guide](/guides/publishing). To publish **Public** your team and work need a public address; the wizard tells you if one is missing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publishing Options">
    - **Version Tag (optional)** — a label such as `v1.0.0`, `beta`, or `final` to identify this release later.
    - **Create Backup** — save a snapshot before publishing (recommended, on by default). A backup is what lets you restore an earlier publish.
    - **Send Notifications** — notify your team members about the publish (on by default).
    - **Invalidate cache** — a technical option (on by default) that clears cached copies of the work; leave it on unless support asks otherwise.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm & Publish">
    Review the summary (work, target, tag, options) and click **Publish Now**. A progress screen tracks the stages.
  </Step>
</Steps>

On success the heading tells you what happened. For **Public** and **Review link** you get the reader link (copy and open), the embed code, a QR code and sharing buttons; the raw manifest URL is tucked away under **Technical details**. For **Internal preview** you get **Open internal preview**.

The size figures are available any time without publishing: the **Metadata** tab of Work Properties has a read-only **Size &amp; Content** section showing the manifest size, the asset count and payload, and the total — useful for keeping an eye on a work's weight as it grows.

<Callout kind="tip">
  Publishing keeps running on the server even if you close the wizard mid-way. Reopening the wizard for the same work within a few minutes reattaches to the running publish and continues showing its progress.
</Callout>

If publishing fails, the wizard shows the error with a **Try Again** button and a **Copy error details** button for support requests.

## What readers get

A publish freezes the work's **manifest** (`panelwave.json`) — the [open-format](/schema/overview) description of your chapters, panels, graph, and settings — as a snapshot. Your artwork and audio stay in PanelWave storage and are linked from the manifest with short-lived signed URLs that are issued each time the work is opened. Later edits stay private until you publish again.

The published manifest is the lean **player version** of your work:

- panels are keyed by their short **friendly ids** (`ch1-p022`), never by internal database ids;
- editor-only state (layer names and sizes, lock/visibility toggles, balloon tail geometry, grid helpers), the translation-workflow data, asset-library folders, and assets that no panel uses are left out;
- strings in languages the work does not offer are dropped;
- images ship as the full-size WebP rendition (or the optimized version) and videos as the H.264 rendition, with the responsive sizes alongside.

Paywalled parts are removed from the public version on the server, so they cannot be fetched without a matching entitlement. The full-fidelity version with everything the editor needs is what the [work archive](/cms/export#archive-export) contains.

Content you keep in draft inside the CMS — for example extras marked **Draft (hidden from readers)** — is excluded from the published manifest (see [Extras](/cms/extras)).

## History and restore

**Work Properties → Publishing** lists your latest publishes. **Restore** puts an earlier successful publish back. See the [guide](/guides/publishing#unpublish-history-and-rollback).
