The **Preview** screen lets you experience your work the way a reader will — inside the same PanelWave Player that powers published works — without leaving the CMS. It is a **device lab**: pick devices, a language and a reader, set story variables, and watch what the player does, live.

## Opening the preview

Preview is in the **Ship** group of a work's Backstage rail. You also reach it from the work card's menu or the section grid in the [Works](/cms/works) inspector, and from **Preview** in the editor header.

A work with nothing playable yet — no chapters, or chapters without any panel — shows **"Nothing to preview yet"** with what to add instead of a player error. Once at least one chapter has panels, the preview plays those chapters and notes how many *chapters without panels* it left out.

<Callout kind="info">
  Preview is a private testing surface; readers never see it. By default it renders the **working copy** — your current editing state, including content you have not published yet.
</Callout>

## The three zones

The page has **Setup** on the left, the **stage** in the middle and the **Live state** inspector on the right. On wide screens all three are columns; on narrower ones Setup becomes a row above the stage and the inspector a drawer (<kbd>]</kbd> toggles it, <kbd>[</kbd> the Setup list, <kbd>?</kbd> the Guide rail).

## Setup

### Source

| Source | What the player shows |
|---|---|
| **Working copy** | Your current editing state |
| **Internal preview** | The last internal preview release (listed once one exists) |
| **Staging** | The frozen staging snapshot behind your [review link](/guides/publishing#review-links) |
| **Production** | The frozen snapshot readers get |

Releases are snapshots, so what you see is what reviewers or readers get. See [Publishing](/cms/publishing).

### Devices

Tick any number of devices — they are shown **side by side** on the stage (up to three on a very wide screen, two on a wide one, one below; Setup tells you how many are hidden at the current width). Each device is a real screen size; the player inside believes it has that size and lays the work out for the matching **page format**, which Setup shows next to each device:

| Device | Size | Page format |
|---|---|---|
| **Phone** portrait / landscape | 393 × 852 | Mobile portrait / Desktop landscape |
| **Tablet** portrait / landscape | 1024 × 1366 | Tablet portrait / Desktop landscape |
| **Desktop** (default) | 1920 × 1080 | Desktop landscape |
| **Big screen** | 3840 × 2160 | Bigscreen landscape |
| **A4 print** | 794 × 1123 | A4 portrait, opened in page view — the same rendering the [PDF export](/cms/export) uses |
| **Custom** | any width × height | your choice, or automatic |

A device whose page format the work has no pages for is flagged; the player then falls back to the closest format. Formats are added under [Work Properties → Output](/cms/works#output). Keys <kbd>1</kbd>, <kbd>2</kbd>, <kbd>3</kbd> switch to a single phone, tablet or desktop; <kbd>R</kbd> rotates every device.

### Locale

The **Locale** list offers the work's own languages (default first), so you can check translations, text fit and balloon layout per language. Press <kbd>L</kbd> to jump to it. See [Localization](/cms/localization) for managing translations.

### Entitlement

Choose the reader you are testing as: the **tiers** your [paywall rules](/cms/monetization) name (plus *free*), the **products** a reader may have bought (products a rule requires are listed first), and whether the reader is **age-verified**. Changes apply live — an open paywall closes the moment the reader passes. This is how you test paywalls: preview as *free* to confirm the lock appears where you expect, then add the tier and confirm it unlocks.

<Callout kind="tip">
  The [Entitlement](/cms/monetization#simulating-reader-access) lab and the [paywall map](/cms/monetization#the-content-map) can open Preview pre-configured for a reader persona — the stage then shows a **"Previewing as …"** badge.
</Callout>

### Options

**Autoplay**, **Muted**, **Reduced motion**, **Show cover**, **View** (Auto — chapters with a [canvas layout](/cms/editor/canvas-layout) open in canvas view —, Panel view or Canvas view) and **Seconds per panel**. All apply live.

### Start values

Every variable your work defines (see [Variables](/cms/editor/variables)) gets an input matched to its type — checkbox, dropdown, number or date picker, text. Start values seed the story when it (re)starts; changing one does not restart the story, the player simply takes the new value. **Manage** opens the Variables page, whose **Try it** button comes back here with a value preset.

### Scenarios

**Scenarios** save a complete Setup — devices, rotation, language, entitlement, start values and every option — so you can re-test recurring situations in one click. Built-ins: *Phone free reader*, *Free tier*, *Premium reader*, *Phone + desktop*, *A4 print page*. Type a name into **Save current as…** to add your own; saved scenarios can be deleted. The last Setup is restored on your next visit. **Reset** returns every control to its default and restarts the frames.

## The stage

The frames **fit the stage** at a common scale, so their relative sizes stay true; **50 %** and **100 %** are fixed zooms (drag the stage background to pan). Navigation in one frame **mirrors to the others** — move to the next panel on the phone and the desktop follows — so you see the phone and desktop composition of the same moment at once.

- **Fullscreen** (<kbd>F</kbd>; <kbd>Esc</kbd> leaves) fills your screen with the stage.
- **Restart story** starts the frames over with the current Setup.
- **Share scenario link** copies a URL with the whole Setup — send it to a teammate (they need CMS access) and they see exactly what you see. **Open in new window** opens the same link.

## Live state

The inspector shows what the player is doing, fed by the frame you last interacted with (or the one you pick in its frame switch):

- **Position** — chapter, page and panel with a thumbnail, the view mode and language, and **Open in editor** on that panel.
- **Variables (live)** — the running values, editable in place. Edits reach every frame **without restarting** the story: the quickest way to walk a branch.
- **History / Next** — the last 20 panels and the possible next panels from the current one; click to jump there.
- **Status** — load time, the paywall gate the reader is held at (rule and reason), the preload queue, the reader's entitlement tokens and the player's last errors.
- **Events** — the last 200 player events across frames, with a text filter and an event-name filter; errors are flagged.

## Previewing extras

Bonus content (covers, concept art, wallpapers, …) has its own reader-style preview: the [Extras](/cms/extras#reader-view) page's **Reader view** shows your published extras grouped exactly as readers will see them, drafts excluded.
