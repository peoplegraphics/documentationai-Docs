---
title: Connect ChatGPT
description: Add PanelWave as a custom MCP connector in ChatGPT, sign in to PanelWave, and let ChatGPT read and edit your works.
---

ChatGPT connects to remote MCP servers through **custom connectors**, which you create in **developer mode**. You add the PanelWave address, sign in to PanelWave, and choose what ChatGPT may do.

<Callout kind="alert">
  ChatGPT's developer mode is meant for trusted, advanced use and is only available on some ChatGPT plans. OpenAI changes its menus often; if a step looks different, search OpenAI's help center for "developer mode" and "MCP connectors".
</Callout>

## Add the connector

<Steps>
  <Step title="Turn on developer mode">
    In ChatGPT, open **Settings → Apps & Connectors → Advanced settings** and switch on **Developer mode**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the connector">
    Back in **Apps & Connectors**, choose **Create**. Name it **PanelWave**, enter the MCP server URL and choose **OAuth** as the authentication:

    ```
    https://mcp.panelwave.org/mcp
    ```
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in to PanelWave">
    ChatGPT opens a PanelWave page. Sign in, check the requested permissions on **Connect an app to PanelWave**, untick anything you don't want, and click **Allow**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use it in a chat">
    In a new chat, choose developer mode from the tools menu and switch on the PanelWave connector. Then ask: "Which PanelWave works do I have?"
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Working with ChatGPT

ChatGPT sees the same tools as any other assistant. Ask for one step at a time, and check the result in the PanelWave editor before you continue. ChatGPT asks you to confirm before it runs a tool that writes data; PanelWave additionally asks before anything destructive, such as deleting a panel or publishing to production.

Examples:

- "Show me the outline of my work *Rooftop*."
- "Add three speech balloons to panel 4: Mira says 'Look up.', Tom answers 'At what?', and a caption reads 'Midnight.'"
- "Which balloons are not translated into German yet?"

ChatGPT can't upload files from your computer into PanelWave. Upload artwork in the editor, or use an assistant with the [local bridge](/cms/integrations/local-bridge).

## Disconnecting

Open **Profile → Connected apps & access tokens** in PanelWave and click **Disconnect** next to ChatGPT. Deleting the connector in ChatGPT only removes it on ChatGPT's side.
