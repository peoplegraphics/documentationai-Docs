The CMS tells you when something happens that concerns you — a comment on one of your works, an export that finished, a publish that went through, an invitation to a team. Notifications arrive in three places that always agree with each other:

- the **bell** in the top bar of every screen, with the number of unread notifications;
- the **Inbox** on the [Dashboard](/cms/dashboard#inbox), with your eight newest unread ones;
- the **Notifications** page (**See all** in the Inbox, or the bell).

The bell checks for new notifications about once a minute and whenever you return to the tab.

## The Notifications page

The page lists everything, newest first. Unread rows are highlighted; clicking a row marks it read and takes you to the place it is about — the comment thread, the export, the published work, the team page. **Unread only** narrows the list, **Mark all read** clears every badge at once and **Load more** fetches the next 25.

## What you are notified about

| Topic | When |
|---|---|
| **Comments** | A team member comments on one of your works, a comment is held for review, you are mentioned, or a moderator acts on your comment. See [team comments](/cms/community#team-comments-in-the-cms). |
| **Exports** | An export job finished or failed. See [Export](/cms/export). |
| **Publishing** | A publish succeeded, failed, or was rolled back. See [Publishing](/cms/publishing). |
| **Team** | You were invited to a team or joined one, a new member joined your team, the team's plan changed, a Work Pass was bought, or the team was suspended or reactivated. See [Account & Team](/cms/account). |
| **Plan limits** | An action was stopped by a [plan limit](/cms/plans) — for example one publication too many. |
| **Speech generation** | A batch of generated voice takes is done. See [Audio localization](/cms/localization#audio-localization-and-text-to-speech). |

Notifications are personal: each member of a team gets their own, and reading one does not change what teammates see.

## Email

In-app notifications do not send email. Email for **comments and mentions on your works** is a separate switch under **Profile → Preferences → Email notifications**, next to the product-news setting — see [Account & Team](/cms/account#preferences).
