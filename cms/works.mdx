**Works** (`/works`) is your library: every work of your team, ready to find, compare and manage. Every interactive graphic novel you author is a **work** — a self-contained project with its own chapters, assets, characters, translations, and settings. (After signing in you land on the [Dashboard](/cms/dashboard); **Works** is one click away in the left rail.)

## Creating a work

Open **+ New** and choose **New work** to start the four-step wizard. On a wide screen it slides in as a sheet on the right, over the library; on a smaller one it is a dialog.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Basic Info">
    Enter the **Work Title** and the **URL Slug** (lowercase letters, numbers, and hyphens; auto-generated from the title). The wizard checks slug availability live — you'll see **Available** or **Already taken**.

    Optionally pick a starter template (**Webcomic**, **Branching Novel**, **All-Ages Story**, **Graphic Novel** — each shown as an illustrated tile) to pre-fill languages, age rating, tags, and page formats — you can still adjust everything in the next steps. You can also add a short **description** and upload a **cover** right here (both can be changed later in the work's properties), and members of several teams pick the **Team** the work belongs to.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Languages">
    Choose the **Default Language** and tick any **Additional Languages**. The list is grouped by region and searchable; **Select all** and **Clear** help with large selections. You can add or remove languages later in the [Localization Workshop](/cms/localization).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Page Formats">
    Pick every **page format** — screen or print shape — the work targets: from **Mobile portrait** and **Tablet portrait** through **Desktop**, **Big screen**, and **Square** to the print and video shapes (**A4**, **US Letter**, **Video 16:9**, **Flex landscape**). Pages are laid out separately per format, so select only what you actually plan to design for.

    One selected card is badged **Default** — that format is used for previews and exports. The first format you select becomes the default automatically; **Make default** on any other selected card changes it, and the default itself cannot be deselected. You can add or remove formats later in [Work Properties → Output](#output).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Details">
    Pick an **Age Rating** — **Everyone** (all ages), **Teen** (13+), **Mature** (17+), or **Adult** (18+) — and add optional **Tags** (press Enter, comma, or **Add**). Tick **Start with a first chapter** to have "Chapter 1" created with an empty **Page 1 in every selected format**, so the editor isn't empty in any of them. Click **Create Work**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="tip">
If you close the wizard halfway, your input is kept as a draft — next time you'll see a **Resume where you left off?** banner with **Resume** and **Discard**.
</Callout>

The same **+ New** menu starts from existing material instead of a blank work: **Import JSON** loads a PanelWave manifest, **Import archive** restores a complete work archive with all its media (see [Import](/cms/import)), **Import script** turns a Fountain screenplay into a work with AI-suggested panels and speech bubbles (see [Script Import](/cms/script-import)), and **Import pages** has AI split finished page images into panels (see [Page Import](/cms/page-import)).

## Three views of your library

The toolbar's view toggle switches between three views of the same works:

- **Grid** (default) — cards with the whole cover at its real aspect ratio in a fixed-height box (portrait covers are never cropped), the status chip, a **Live** chip with a **Read** link for published works, a progress ring (panels with artwork vs. total), chapter/panel/character counts, language flags, readers in the last 30 days, and when and by whom the work was last edited. The number of columns follows your screen — six on a 4K monitor, three on a laptop.
- **Wall** — pure art: covers at their real aspect ratio in justified rows, title and one status line underneath, controls only on hover.
- **Table** — one row per work: Cover · Title · Team · Status · Published · Progress · Chapters · Panels · Languages · Formats · Updated · Last edited by · Readers (30 d) · Actions. Sort by any column, pick the columns you want with **Columns** (remembered for you), scroll for more — in compact density thirty-plus works fit on one screen.

Your choice is remembered. On a small screen the library falls back to a compact list.

## Browsing and filtering

- **Search** by title, slug, or work ID (<kbd>/</kbd> focuses it).
- **Team** — members of several teams see a team select: one team, or **All teams**. The choice is shared with the [Dashboard](/cms/dashboard) and remembered across devices.
- **Status** (All, Draft, Review, Published, Archived), **Language**, **Format** and **Tag** filters — the last three offer what the works on the page actually use.
- **Sort by** Updated (default), Created, Title, Progress or Published, ascending or descending.
- Two tabs: **Works** (active) and **Trash**.

Single-click a work to see it in the inspector; double-click (or <kbd>Enter</kbd>, <kbd>o</kbd>, <kbd>e</kbd>) opens it in the editor; middle-click opens it in a new tab. The arrow keys move through cards or rows, <kbd>]</kbd> toggles the inspector, <kbd>?</kbd> the Guide rail.

## The inspector

The right column (a drawer on narrower screens) shows everything about the selected work on its **Overview** tab: the cover with its status, the title in every language, the slug with a copy button, series and issue, age rating, creators with their roles, the progress ring with chapters, pages, panels with artwork, assets, characters, paywall rules and likes, the **publishing state** with the production and review links (copy with one click, open the public page), a readers sparkline for the last 30 days, the description, publisher, release date, tags and content warnings, languages (with the default marked), page formats (with the default marked), reading direction, team, created/updated/last edit — and a **section grid** that jumps to any Backstage tool of the work: Editor, Graph Editor, Canvas Layout, Characters, Extras, Variables, Localization, Audio, Preview, Validation, Paywall, Entitlement, Comments, Analytics. **Open editor**, **Export…**, the status select and **Move to Trash** are here too.

The **Properties** tab edits the work's settings in place — see [Work Properties](#metadata) below.

## Work statuses

A work is always in one of four statuses, changeable from the status select in the inspector or with a bulk action:

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Draft** | Work in progress |
| **Review** | Ready for internal review |
| **Published** | Live for readers (see [Publishing](/cms/publishing)) |
| **Archived** | Kept, but out of the way |

## The card menu

The **⋮ More actions** button on a card (or table row) opens a menu with **Editor**, **Open in new tab**, every Backstage section of the work, **Export…** (opens the [export wizard](/cms/export#the-export-wizard) for that work), **Open public page** and **Copy reader link** for published works, **Properties** (see below) and **Move to Trash**.

The **Export…** button in the toolbar opens the export wizard too; ticked works are preselected.

## Bulk actions

Tick the checkbox on one or more cards or rows (or **Select all**) to reveal the bulk action bar:

- **Move to Trash** — trash all selected works.
- **Set status** — change the status of all selected works at once.
- **Export…** — open the [export wizard](/cms/export#the-export-wizard) for the selected works: PDF, PowerPoint, Video, Archive (.zip), or Package with player (.zip).

In the Trash view, the bulk bar offers **Restore** and **Delete permanently** instead.

## Trash, restoring, and deleting

**Move to Trash** never deletes anything immediately. Switch to the **Trash** tab to **Restore** works or delete them for good; every trashed work shows when it was trashed and how many days are left before it is deleted automatically (30 days). Permanent deletion opens a confirmation dialog — **Delete Work Permanently?** — because it removes all chapters, panels, assets, and data and **cannot be undone**. The work's files (artwork, audio, video and their generated variants) are removed from storage in a nightly cleanup **7 days** after the deletion; files another work still uses — for example a portrait shared through the [team character library](/cms/characters#team-character-library) — are kept.

<Callout kind="alert">
There is currently no duplicate-work action on the works list. To copy a work, [export](/cms/export) it as JSON and [import](/cms/import) it under a new slug.
</Callout>

## Work Properties — work-level settings and defaults

In the library, select a work and open the inspector's **Properties** tab: the **Metadata**, **Localization** and **Output** settings are edited right there, with an explicit **Save**. The remaining tabs — **Typography**, **Audio/Visual**, **Video Defaults** and **Publishing** — still open the **Work Properties** dialog (**Open full properties**, or the tab's own button). Inside the editor the dialog opens from the work node's context menu in the [structure tree](/cms/editor/pages-panels#context-menus) or with <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>,</kbd>. The sections:

### Metadata
Localized **Name** and **Description** (the fields offer the work's own languages; the **Name** is the work's title, so renaming it here changes the published title and the title in the Localization grid), **Series Name** and **Issue / Episode Number**, a **Cover Image** picker (choose from the work's uploaded image assets; the preview shows the whole cover, uncropped), **Creators**, and publishing fields: **Publisher**, **Release Date**, **Age Rating**, **Tags / Genres**, **Copyright**, and **License**.

### Typography
Work-wide visual defaults:

- **Default Font** and **Default Font Size**, plus the **Default Page Background Color**.
- **Speech Balloon Style (Work Default)** — the balloon configurator with live preview. This is the base style for *every* speech bubble in the work; [characters](/cms/characters), presets and individual bubbles can override it (styles cascade: work default → character override → balloon preset → per-bubble override).
- **Balloon Presets** — named balloon styles (*caption*, *voice-over*, *shout*, …) that a bubble picks in the Speech inspector. Add one by name, style it with the same configurator, delete it with the bin icon. See [Balloon presets](/cms/editor/speech-bubbles#balloon-presets).
- **Panel Border Style** (color, thickness, solid/dashed/none) and the **Gutter Width** between panels. The gutter is also the spacing [Arrange by Artwork](/cms/editor/pages-panels#arranging-pages-by-artwork) leaves between panels.

Saving Work Properties keeps every typography setting this tab doesn't show, such as the text-style presets of an imported manifest.

### Localization
The **Default Locale**, the set of **Supported Locales**, and the **Reading Direction**. These are the same locales managed in the [Localization Workshop](/cms/localization) — a change in either place shows up in the other.

Locales are full BCP-47 codes shown with their names — *English (US) (en-US)*, *German (de-DE)* — the same codes the workshop, the editor, and the published manifest use. The list offers the work's own locales (default first) plus the shared locale catalogue. The localized **Name** and **Description** on the Metadata tab are stored under these codes too. Works that were saved with bare language codes (`en`, `de`) before 2026-09-25 have been converted to the work's matching locale (or the catalogue default for that language), so nothing needs re-entering.

The number of supported locales is capped by your [plan](/cms/plans); the default locale must be one of the supported ones.

### Audio/Visual
Default **Ambient**, **SFX**, and **Voice** volume levels, and the default panel **Transition Effect** and **Transition Duration**.

### Output
The set of **Supported Page Formats** and which of them is the **Default**:

- Every supported format appears in the editor's [format switcher](/cms/editor/canvas), and each format keeps its own set of page layouts.
- One format is badged **Default** — it is used for previews and exports and cannot be removed; click **Make default** on another supported format first.
- **Adding a format** asks whether to also create an empty first page in the new format for each chapter, so the editor isn't empty there. Chapters that already have pages in that format are skipped.
- **Removing a format** that still has pages shows a warning first: the pages are **kept**, but no longer shown in the editor or included in exports until you re-add the format. Nothing is deleted.

<Callout kind="info">
Works created before supported formats existed have no stored selection — they support **all** formats until you save an explicit list here.
</Callout>

### Video Defaults
Defaults applied to new video layers (view mode, start mode, **Muted by default**) unless overridden per layer — see [Video Panels](/cms/editor/video-panels).

Click **Save** (in the inspector) or **Save Properties** (in the dialog) to apply. Values outside their allowed range (for example a font size outside 8–72, a gutter above 50, or a transition longer than 5 seconds) are rejected with a list of what to correct. On a [read-only work](/cms/plans#read-only-works) the properties can be opened but not saved.
