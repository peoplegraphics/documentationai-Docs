Sound turns a graphic novel into an experience: rain behind a scene, a theme that carries the whole story, an explosion exactly when the panel shows it. PanelWave gives you two kinds of audio:

- **Sound effects on panels**: sounds that belong to a single panel and start when the reader reaches it (an explosion, a door knock, a line of dialogue).
- **Timeline audio tracks**: music and ambience that run across panels. A track lasts for the **whole work**, a **chapter**, a **page**, or a custom stretch of time.

Both are built from audio **assets** you upload once and reuse anywhere.

## Getting audio into your work

Upload audio files in the [Assets](/cms/assets) tab (drag and drop or the upload button). The editor accepts common audio formats — MP3, WAV, OGG, AAC, and FLAC all play in the editor preview.

When you upload, the file's **duration is detected automatically** and stored with the asset, so tracks can default to the natural length of the sound. While a file is still being prepared you'll see a **Processing…** indicator on its asset card; it becomes usable as soon as processing finishes in the background.

<Callout kind="tip">
  Use the **Audio** filter in the Assets tab to see only your sound files. Asset cards show the duration and file size, which helps when picking between takes.
</Callout>

## Sound effects on panels

A panel's sound effects start when the reader reaches the panel, optionally after a delay, and stop when the reader moves on. There are three quick ways to add one:

- **Drag and drop**: drag an audio file from the Assets tab onto a panel on the canvas, or onto the timeline. On the timeline it lands on the panel under the pointer, starting at the point where you dropped it.
- **Timeline**: select a panel and click **+ Sound Effect** in the timeline header (or the **+** next to *Panel SFX*). The sound starts at the playhead if the playhead is inside that panel.
- **Inspector**: select a panel and click **+ Add Sound Effect** in the **Audio Tracks** section.

All panel sounds appear in the timeline's **Panel SFX** lane, at the panel's start plus their delay:

- **Click** a sound to open it in the editor dialog.
- **Drag** it left or right to change when it starts within its panel. With the keyboard, focus it and press ← / → to move it by 100 ms.
- A sound longer than what is left of its panel shows a dashed right edge. The reader leaves the panel before it ends, so the editor cuts it there, just like the reader does.
- Overlapping sounds stack in extra rows.

### Sound effect options

| Field | What it does |
|---|---|
| Role | SFX (default), Ambient, Music, Voiceover, or UI. The reader's **SFX** switch mutes SFX and UI sounds |
| Starts after (ms) | Delay after the reader reaches the panel |
| Duration Override (ms) | Optional length for the editor preview — leave empty to use the file's length |
| Loop Track | Repeats the sound while the reader stays on the panel |
| Volume | 0–100% playback volume |
| Muted | Keeps the sound in the editor but leaves it out of exports and the published work |

The Inspector's **Audio Tracks** list shows each sound's role and delay, with quick mute, edit, and remove buttons.

## Timeline audio tracks

Timeline tracks are for music and ambience that keep playing while the reader (or the autoplay playhead) moves from panel to panel.

### Creating a track

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick the place">
    Select a panel (or a page or chapter). "This chapter" and "This page" refer to the selection, or to the panel under the playhead when nothing is selected.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the dialog">
    Click **+ Audio Track** in the timeline header.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the sound">
    Click **Select Audio Asset** and pick a file. You can listen to it right in the dialog. The file name becomes the track name; change it if you like.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set role and length">
    Choose the **Role** (Music, Ambient, Voiceover, SFX) and the **Length**, then click **Create Track**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Length

| Length | The track plays… |
|---|---|
| Whole work | From the first panel to the last panel of the work, across all chapters |
| This chapter | From the first to the last panel of the current chapter |
| This page | Over the panels of the current page |
| Custom | From a start time (relative to the chapter's start) for a set duration — or the file's own length |

The dialog previews where the range starts, how long it lasts, and how many panels it covers. Work, chapter, and page tracks **follow their range**: when you change panel durations or reading order, the track grows or shrinks with them.

<Callout kind="tip">
  If the file is shorter than the range, the dialog suggests **Loop Track** so the music or ambience doesn't stop halfway. A new track turns it on automatically. Once you change Loop yourself, the dialog stops suggesting it.
</Callout>

The other options — volume, fade in/out, playback rate (0.5×–2.0×), muted — work as you'd expect. Tracks are created for the device format you're editing.

### In the timeline

Each track has its own lane, color-coded by role, with a **Work**, **Chapter**, or **Page** badge when it follows a range:

| Role | Typical use |
|---|---|
| Ambient | Environmental atmosphere — rain, wind, room tone |
| Music | Background music and themes |
| Voiceover | Narration and dialogue |
| SFX | Sound effects that span several panels |

- **Click a clip** (or press Enter on it) to edit the track.
- Each lane's label has a **mute** and a **delete** button.
- Clips show extra indicators when the volume differs from 100%, when looping is on, and shaded ramps for fades.

With many lanes, the timeline scrolls vertically. Drag the timeline's **top edge** (or focus it and press ↑ / ↓) to make it taller.

### Seeing them per panel

Select any panel and check the Inspector's **Sequence Audio Tracks** section: it lists exactly the timeline tracks that play during that panel, with their start and duration, plus quick mute, edit, and delete buttons.

## Previewing audio in the editor

Press **Play** in the [Timeline panel](/cms/editor/animations) to hear your mix:

- Music, ambience, and panel sound effects play **together and in sync**. Every sound starts exactly when it is due, even one that begins long after you pressed Play. Each sound stops at its end.
- Volumes, fades, playback rate, and looping are applied as configured. Muted tracks and sounds stay silent.
- **Seeking** (clicking the ruler or dragging the playhead) and looping restart the mix at the right position.
- The canvas follows the playhead from panel to panel, so picture and sound stay together.

<Callout kind="info">
  Browsers only allow sound after you interact with the page — that's why playback always starts from the Play button. If you hear nothing on the very first attempt, click Play again.
</Callout>

For the full reader-side behavior of audio (mixing rules, autoplay policies in the published player), see [Player audio](/player/audio).

<Callout kind="info">
  In the published player, timeline tracks follow the reader: music starts at the right point when the reader reaches its panels, keeps playing while they read on, and fades out when they leave its range; **Whole work** tracks carry on across chapters. This needs the player release after 1.5.0 — the editor preview already uses it.
</Callout>

## Localized audio

Like text, audio assets can vary per language — a German voiceover for `de-DE` readers, English for `en-US`. Manage per-locale recordings in the [Localization](/cms/localization) workshop; the format details are in [Audio in the schema](/schema/audio) and [Localization concepts](/concepts/localization).
