**Extras** are bonus content that readers can browse alongside your story: covers, concept art, author interviews, behind-the-scenes material. Open **Extras** from a work's Backstage rail (group **Cast & Content**).

## The bonus shelf

The page has three zones: the **bucket rail** on the left, the **shelf** in the middle and the **inspector** on the right (a column on wide screens, a drawer on narrower ones — <kbd>]</kbd> toggles it, <kbd>[</kbd> the rail, <kbd>?</kbd> the Guide rail).

### Buckets

Extras are organized into eight buckets, each with an icon, its item count, its gated count and a dot when it holds drafts; **All** at the top shows everything across buckets:

| Bucket | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Cover** | Cover art as browsable content (see below — not the same as the work's metadata cover) |
| **Alt Covers** | Alternative cover art — add as many as you like; readers see all of them |
| **Character Sheets** | Character reference sheets (each linked to one or more [characters](/cms/characters)) |
| **Author Info** | About the author |
| **Interviews** | Author interviews |
| **Bonus Art** | Bonus artwork and panels |
| **Fan Art** | Community fan art |
| **Behind the Scenes** | Process, sketches, soundtrack |

**Drag an extra onto another bucket** to move it there. The rail's footer sums up the work — items, gated, drafts, views, downloads — and holds the **Reader view** toggle. On narrower screens the rail becomes a row of icon tabs above the shelf.

### The two covers

PanelWave has two distinct "covers", and the Cover bucket is the less important one:

- The **work cover** (set in [Work Properties](/cms/works#metadata)) is the canonical one — it appears on work cards, in libraries and social embeds, and it's what preflight's "missing cover" check looks at.
- The **Cover extra** in this bucket is optional bonus content: cover art readers can view full-size in the Extras section, optionally shareable, downloadable, or gated, with **Alt Covers** as siblings.

An empty Cover bucket offers **Use work cover** (creates the cover extra from the work cover), and the inspector of an asset-linked cover extra offers **Set as work cover**.

## The shelf

The default **Wall** shows every item at its real aspect ratio in justified rows — portrait covers are never cropped — with chips for **gated** (lock and tier) and **draft** always visible and **shareable** / **downloadable** on hover. Clicking a chip toggles it right there. **Grid** shows classic cards; **List** is a sortable table (thumbnail · title · type · size · dimensions · status · gated/tier · share/download · views · downloads · updated).

- **Search** and the **Status**, **Gated** and **Type** filters narrow the shelf (<kbd>/</kbd> focuses the search).
- **Reorder** by dragging — across the whole bucket, no paging; the order is what readers see.
- **Select several** with click, <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>/<kbd>⌘</kbd>-click, <kbd>Shift</kbd> for a range or <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>A</kbd>; the **bulk bar** then gates them (with a tier) or frees them, publishes or drafts them, moves them to another bucket or deletes them. <kbd>Del</kbd> deletes the selection, <kbd>Esc</kbd> clears it.

## Adding extras

The quickest way: **drop files anywhere on the shelf**. Each file is uploaded to the work's [asset library](/cms/assets) and becomes a **draft** extra in the current bucket, ready to be titled and published in the inspector.

**+ Add** offers the same and more: **Upload files**, **From library** (the [asset picker](/cms/assets#picking-assets-anywhere) — choose an existing asset or upload one right there), **From URL** (content hosted elsewhere; the address must start with `http(s)://`, `/` or `./`) and **Text** (you write the content directly). URL and text extras open in the inspector as a draft; click **Create extra** once they have a title.

<Callout kind="info">
Prefer a **library asset** over pasting its URL: the extra stays connected to the asset, so **Replace File** in the [Assets library](/cms/assets) updates it automatically, and readers always receive a fresh, working link. External URLs remain fully supported for content hosted outside PanelWave.
</Callout>

## The inspector

Selecting an item shows it in the inspector — a **preview fitted to the column** (images zoom on click, video and audio play natively, PDFs render inline, text is shown formatted) — above a form that **saves as you type**; a *Saving… / Saved* line confirms it. Toggles and selects save immediately.

- **Title** and **Description** with one **tab per language**, each marked ✓ or ✗ so you can see which translations are still missing. The default language is the fallback for the others. See [Localization](/cms/localization).
- **Type** — Image, Video, Audio, PDF or Text — **Status** and **Bucket**.
- **Content** — the linked asset as a chip with **Replace** and **Unlink**, or the external URL (with a **Library** button to swap it for an asset), or the text editor. Plus the **Poster** (video) or **Thumbnail** (everything else), optional.
- **Gated** — the extra is locked behind a purchase. The **Required tier** is picked from the tiers your [products](/cms/monetization#products-price-points-and-coupons) actually sell; a hint tells you which product unlocks it. **Shareable** lets readers share a link to it; **Downloadable** lets them download the file.
- **Characters** — required in the **Character Sheets** bucket: tick one character for a single sheet or several for an ensemble sheet; they appear with their portraits in the order you ticked them. Sheets without a linked character are dropped from the export.
- **Facts** — dimensions or duration (read-only when they come from the asset), size, file type, whether the content is a library asset, an external URL or inline text, views and downloads, created and updated.
- **Set as work cover** for an asset-linked cover extra, and **Delete**.

## Draft vs. published

**Draft (hidden from readers)** keeps an extra visible on the shelf but out of the published work, the export and the reader view. The workflow: create as draft → review in **Reader view** → flip to **Published** → the extra is included the next time you [publish](/cms/publishing) or [export](/cms/export).

## Reader view

The **Reader view** toggle in the rail turns the shelf into the extras section as readers see it: grouped by bucket, drafts excluded, with a **language** switcher for localized titles and descriptions and locked items shown as locked with their tier. Toggle it off to return to the shelf.

Extras end up in the `extras` section of the manifest — see the [Extras schema reference](/schema/extras).
